About this collection

Five supplied Spotify CSV exports, covering 156 playlist entries. Playlist names are curator labels; they are not independently verified track genres.

Cleaning and validation

Checked all 19 columns, Spotify track identifiers, artist name / ID alignment, positive durations, explicit flags, recording codes and dates. Removed hyphens from two ISRC recording codes and logged the original values. No duplicate track IDs were found within playlists; no entries were removed. Original CSV files remain unchanged.

Reading the metrics

An entry is one row in one playlist. The same recording can occur across playlists. Recording overlap uses distinct normalized ISRCs; these are identifiers, not audio fingerprints. Primary artist means the first credited artist. Album charts group by title. Album release dates can reflect reissues rather than original releases.

Artist variety is distinct primary artists per 100 entries. It is sensitive to playlist size. Jungle / DnB has only four entries; its concentration and variety should be interpreted in that context. Duration totals describe available music, not listening time.

Timeline and scope

The calendar uses the UTC Added At field. It counts retained entries by addition date and cannot reconstruct removed songs or historical playlist size. The recent-additions reference date defaults to the latest observed addition. Monthly counts respond to the selected year; the annual timeline shows the full collection range.

No playback, skip, BPM, key, energy or listener data were supplied. The export’s Popularity field is not used as a listening measure. This analysis was prepared with AI assistance.
